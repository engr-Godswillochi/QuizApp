# Neural Signal Lab direction

## THESIS

Prep50 is a midnight learning instrument where eight course areas form a luminous knowledge network. It helps a COS 438 student strengthen connections through repeated, source-aligned practice rather than generic trivia.

## OWN-WORLD

- Cyan signals comprehension and available action.
- Violet signals connections between topics.
- Pink signals attention and the current record.
- Green and red appear only after submission for correct and incorrect states.
- Near-black is the quiet laboratory surrounding the work.
- Manrope carries every interface and reading role with compact, legible geometry.

## STORY

The student enters a ready learning system, starts a randomized 50-question path through the material, watches the numbered signal grid fill, submits with an explicit unanswered count, then reviews topic performance and explanations. The product name and Q mark remain Prep50 across every state.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

A compact course rail establishes the 200-question bank, eight topics, and 50-question attempt. The main stage pairs the promise “Train the connections” with an immediate start action and a live network of the course domains. On mobile, the rail becomes a 66px course bar and the copy, action, and network stack in that order.
